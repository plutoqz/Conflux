# Introduction to ArcGIS Online—ArcGIS Online Help | Documentation

> Source: https://doc.arcgis.com/en/arcgis-online/get-started/what-is-agol.htm

ArcGIS Onlineis a cloud-based mapping and analysis solution. Use it to make maps, to analyze data, and to share and collaborate. Get access to  workflow-specific apps, maps and data from around the globe, and tools for being mobile in the field. Your data and maps are stored in a secure and private infrastructure  and can be configured to meet your mapping and IT requirements.

ArcGIS Online
is a cloud-based mapping and analysis solution. Use it to make maps, to analyze data, and to share and collaborate. ### Manage data

Manage data
Add, manage, and share your own data. You canpublish data as web layersonArcGIS Online. This frees up your internal resources, because these web layers are hosted in theEsricloud and scale dynamically as demand increases or decreases. You can add the web layers to maps and allow others to use them as well. You can publish data directly fromArcGIS ProorArcGIS Onlineand share the data with others.
